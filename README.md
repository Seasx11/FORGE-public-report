**FORGE is the connected workspace for engine rebuilders and high-mix CNC shops.** Every job carries its customer, quote, parts, travelers, time, files and customer updates in one place, so the floor, the front office and your customer all see the same work.

## Who it's for

FORGE is built for shops where the job itself carries the context: what came in, what was agreed, what is waiting and who is waiting on it.

| Engine rebuilders | High-mix CNC shops |
| --- | --- |
| Customer-owned blocks, heads and assemblies | Short runs against drawings and revisions |
| Work that changes as parts are torn down and inspected | Jobs that depend on the right revision and material lot |
| Customers who want to know where their engine is | Customers who need a clear quote before work starts |

It serves shop owners and coordinators who run the work, the team on the floor, and the customers who are waiting on it.

## Get started

FORGE runs in your browser. There is nothing to install.

1. **Sign in** with the invitation for your shop.
2. **Add your customers.**
3. **Open a job** with its status, due date and next action.
4. **Draft a quote** and **issue** it to your customer.
5. **Your customer approves** it from their own sign-in.

The [getting started guide](docs/getting-started.md) walks through each step with screenshots.

## How it works

<img src="assets/diagrams/how-it-works.svg" alt="Your team and your customers work from one FORGE workspace. Each job keeps its parts, travelers, time, files and quote history; customers follow progress and approve quotes in their portal, and each shop's records stay separate." width="100%">

- **One job record.** Customer, scope, parts, travelers, time, files and quote history live together.
- **Two views.** Your team runs the work. Your customer follows it in their portal and approves quotes.
- **Separate by shop.** Each shop's records are kept apart, every account is by invitation, and internal notes and costs never reach the customer view.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need to install anything?</b></summary>

No. FORGE runs in a current web browser on a desktop, tablet or phone.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can my customers see everything in a job?</b></summary>

No. Customers see their own jobs and what you share with them, such as issued quotes and published updates. Drafts, internal notes, costs and other customers' work are never shown.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does approving a quote take a payment?</b></summary>

No. Approval records the customer's acceptance of the quote's scope and amount. It does not make a payment.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I get access?</b></summary>

FORGE is invitation-only during the preview. See [Support and access requests](docs/support.md).
</details>

More answers are in the [full FAQ](docs/faq.md).
