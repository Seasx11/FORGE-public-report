# Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common questions about FORGE and the invited preview.

## About FORGE

**What is FORGE?**
A browser-based workspace for engine rebuilders and high-mix CNC shops. It keeps each job's customer, scope, status, due date, next action and quote history in one place, and gives your customers their own view of their jobs.

**Who is it for?**
Shop owners, coordinators and floor staff at engine rebuilding and CNC job shops, and the customers they do work for.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. FORGE runs in a current web browser on a desktop, tablet or phone.

**Is FORGE open source?**
No. FORGE is proprietary software. This repository holds its public documentation only.

## The preview

**Is FORGE available now?**
FORGE is in an invited preview. Accounts are created by invitation for participating shops and their customers. See [Support](support.md) to request access.

**What does it cost?**
There are no paid plans during the preview.

**What is not available yet?**
Self-service sign-up and password reset, manufacturing workflows, attachments, and email or text messaging.

**Can I import my existing jobs?**
Not in the preview. Customers and jobs are added in FORGE directly.

**Does FORGE email or text my customers?**
No. FORGE does not send messages during the preview. Adding a customer or issuing a quote does not notify anyone outside FORGE.

## Customers and quotes

**Can my customers see everything in a job?**
No. Customers see their own jobs and the quote revisions you issue to them. Drafts and other customers' work are never shown. See [what customers see](guides/customers.md#what-customers-see).

**Does approving a quote take a payment?**
No. Approval records the customer's acceptance of the quote's scope and amount. It does not make a payment.

**Can I change a quote after my customer has seen it?**
Yes. Draft a new revision and issue it. The earlier revision stays on record. See [Change a quote](guides/quotes-and-approvals.md#change-a-quote).

## Data and security

**Is my shop's data kept separate from other shops?**
Yes. Each shop's records are kept apart, and every account is tied to a shop by invitation.

**How long do I stay signed in?**
FORGE signs you out after 30 minutes without activity, and after 8 hours in any case. See [Sessions](account-and-access.md#sessions).
