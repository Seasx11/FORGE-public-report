# Troubleshooting

What each FORGE message means and what to do next. Messages are listed as they appear on screen.

## Signing in

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Identity provider rejected the request.* | The email address or password was not accepted | Check both and try again. For password help, contact the person who invited you |
| *Use your shop-specific invitation link to sign in.* | Your email address belongs to more than one shop | Sign in through the invitation link for the shop you want |
| *No active membership.* | Your account is not active for this shop | Contact the person who invited you |
| *Too many attempts.* | Several sign-in attempts failed in a short time | Wait a few minutes, then try again |
| *Your session ended. Please sign in again.* | You were signed out after 30 minutes without activity, or 8 hours after signing in | Sign in again. Saved work is kept |

Self-service password reset is not available during the preview.

## Jobs and customers

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Add a customer first, then create their job.* | Every job needs a customer, and your shop has none yet | Add the customer in the form FORGE opens, then open the job |
| *Job changed. Refresh before editing.* | Someone else saved this job while you were editing | Refresh the page, review their changes, and edit again |

## Quotes

If the quote form will not save, your browser highlights the field to fix. The unit price takes up to two decimal places, such as `1280` or `1280.00`, and the quantity is a whole number of 1 or more.

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Enter a positive quantity and price with a total no greater than $1,000,000.* | Quantity times unit price is more than $1,000,000 | Lower the quantity or price, or split the work across quotes |
| *Latest draft required.* | Only the newest draft can be issued | Refresh and issue the revision marked **Current** |
| *Latest issued quote required.* | Seen by customers: a newer revision has been drafted since they opened the page | Your customer can approve once you issue the new revision |

## Connection

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Could not connect. Please try again.* | Your browser could not reach FORGE | Check your connection and try again |
| *The request took too long. Refresh to check whether it completed before trying again.* | FORGE did not answer in time | Refresh first. The change may already be saved; this avoids saving it twice |
| *The workspace is unavailable.* | The workspace could not load | Select **Try again**. If it continues, see [Support](support.md) |
