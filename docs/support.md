# Support and access requests

How to request access to FORGE and where to get help.

## Request access

FORGE is invitation-only during the preview. To ask about access for your shop, contact [@Seasx11 on GitHub](https://github.com/Seasx11). Tell us:

- your shop's name and the kind of work you do (engine rebuilding, CNC, or both);
- roughly how many people would sign in.

Please do not include customer names or other personal information.

## Help with your account

For password or account help, contact the person who invited you. Self-service password reset is not available during the preview.

## Help using FORGE

- [Getting started](getting-started.md) walks through the core workflow.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) explains every message you might see.
- The [FAQ](faq.md) answers common questions.

## Security concerns

If you believe you have found a security issue, contact [@Seasx11 on GitHub](https://github.com/Seasx11) privately. Do not describe it in a public issue.
