# Accounts and access

FORGE is invitation-only during the preview. There is no software to install and nothing to configure on your own computers: each shop has its own workspace, and every person who signs in has an account created by invitation.

## Requirements

- A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a desktop, tablet or phone.
- An invitation for your shop.

## Staff and customer accounts

| | Staff | Customer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | People who work at your shop | Your shop's customers |
| Sees | Every job and customer in your shop | Only their own jobs |
| Can | Add customers, open and edit jobs, draft and issue quotes | Review and approve quotes issued to them |
| Never sees | Other shops' records | Quote drafts, other customers' jobs, other shops' records |

## Signing in

Open your invitation link and sign in with your email address and password.

- **More than one shop:** if your email address belongs to more than one shop, sign in through the invitation link for the shop you want. Otherwise FORGE asks you to *use your shop-specific invitation link to sign in*.
- **Password or account help:** self-service password reset is not available during the preview. Contact the person who invited you.
- **Too many attempts:** after repeated failed sign-ins, FORGE pauses further attempts for a short time. Wait a few minutes and try again.

## Sessions

For your security, FORGE signs you out after 30 minutes without activity, and after 8 hours in any case. When that happens you see *Your session ended. Please sign in again.* Anything you had already saved is kept.

Select **Sign out** at the top of any page to end your session on a shared computer.

## Customer accounts

Adding a customer record in FORGE does not create a sign-in for them or send them anything. During the preview, customer accounts are set up by invitation. Once your customer can sign in, they see the jobs assigned to them and the quotes you issue.
